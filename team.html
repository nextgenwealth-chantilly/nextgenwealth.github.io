OUR TEAM

Different strengths. One goal.

NextGen Wealth is powered by a team of students who believe that financial education should start early. From developing educational programs to building our community, each member brings a different perspective and skill set to our mission.

FOUNDER

Mahika Damaraju

Mahika Damaraju is a student at Chantilly High School and founder of NextGen Wealth. As a varsity debater and DECA competitor, she seeks to simplify economic concepts and financial skills to help students gain practical "adulting" knowledge and confidence in real-world financial decision-making.

CO-FOUNDER

Reya Simhadri

Reya Simhadri is a student at George C. Marshall High School dedicated to helping young people build essential life and financial skills. Through her experience competing in DECA's Personal Financial Literacy event, she aims to bridge the gap between traditional education and real-world financial knowledge, helping future generations avoid common financial mistakes and achieve smarter financial success.

SOCIAL MEDIA & RECRUITMENT

Emma Jacob

Emma Jacob is a student at Chantilly High School and leads social media strategy and recruitment for NextGen Wealth. She helps create an engaging and accessible digital presence for financial education, making complex economic ideas easier for students to understand. Through her creativity and communication skills, Emma focuses on building community and expanding the reach of financial literacy among students.

COMMUNITY LEAD

Louane Kang

Louane Kang is a student at George C. Marshall High School passionate about empowering young people through financial literacy and meaningful experiences. As a DECA State Qualifier, she hopes to use her knowledge and leadership experience to help students grow, learn, and feel more confident about their futures.

Strategic Initiatives Coordinator

Ananya Gadwala

Ananya Gadwala is a student at Chantilly High School and leads fundraising efforts and program development for NextGen Wealth. As a DECA state awardee in the Principles of Finance event, she understands the importance of basic financial understanding and how to meaningfully reach audiences. Through her innovation and development, Ananya aims to contribute to a more informed generation of spenders.
